# D3 Enemy Art Prompt Log v0.1

Generation mode: Codex built-in ImageGen (imagegen)

Common art direction:

- Original dark-gothic JRPG enemy
- High-definition pixel art / HD-2D
- Crisp chunky pixel clusters
- Dramatic blue-violet rim lighting
- Front/three-quarter battle view
- One enemy only
- No text, UI, frame, floor, shadow plate, or background
- Exact transparent alpha outside the silhouette

## Merox (formerly Astral Mercury Core)

Create an original transparent-background enemy sprite asset for a dark gothic Japanese browser RPG in high-definition pixel art / HD-2D style. Design a compact liquid-chrome comet beast with a faceted star-shaped core. Its silhouette must immediately communicate a rare experience monster, extreme hardness, and extreme speed. Use silver, ice-blue, white and violet highlights with sharp specular pixels.

## Gilded Hoarder

Create an original transparent-background enemy sprite for a dark gothic Japanese browser RPG in high-definition pixel art / HD-2D style. Design a small armored treasure beetle-gremlin with an oversized ornate golden coin-shell and a stolen-material satchel. Its silhouette must immediately communicate GOLD and stealing. Use antique gold, black iron, crimson accents and a mischievous pose.

## Prism Sentinel

Create a single original transparent-background battle enemy sprite in high-definition pixel art / HD-2D gothic style. Design a floating white-crystal cathedral surveillance automaton with a faceted prism eye and broken halo gears. Use white crystal, pale cyan, silver and restrained violet shadows.

## Chain Reaper

Create one original transparent-background RPG battle enemy sprite in high-definition pixel art / HD-2D gothic style. Design CHAIN REAPER with black iron chains, a torn crimson-black mantle and crescent chain-blade arms. Make the silhouette aggressive and readable at mobile battle size.

## Void Alchemist

Create one original transparent-background RPG battle enemy sprite in high-definition pixel art / HD-2D gothic style. Design VOID ALCHEMIST with a porcelain beaked mask, cathedral robes and luminous glass ampoules. Use midnight blue, violet, cyan fluid light and tarnished silver.

## Fortress Golem

Create an imposing fortress golem in high-definition pixel art / HD-2D gothic style. Its body is a walking castle wall with massive shoulders, battlements, iron braces, blue-violet magical seams and a small glowing core. Make it broad, heavy, extremely durable-looking and readable as a large mobile battle sprite.

## Crimson Behemoth

Create one original transparent-background RPG battle enemy sprite in high-definition pixel art / HD-2D gothic style. Design a massive crimson cathedral behemoth with plated shoulders, black iron horns, fortress-like armor and blue-violet corruption seams. Make it visually distinct from the fortress golem and clearly more ferocious.
